How this review works

Positioning clarity We evaluate how quickly the role, level, and professional direction can be understood.
Structure & readability We review whether the document is easy to scan, well organized, and readable in a first pass.
Impact We look at how clearly the CV/Resume communicates contribution, scope, and signs of impact.
Market alignment We assess how aligned the document is with current hiring language, naming, and role expectations.
ATS readability We consider whether the format supports clear parsing and processing across hiring systems.
Consistency & focus We review whether the document maintains a clear narrative, avoids redundancy, and keeps attention on the most relevant information.

Results are indicative and should be understood as guidance to improve the document, not as a final judgment on a profile. Using this tool does not guarantee participation in a Glofy hiring process and does not imply automatic consideration for open roles.
